About

  • I am currently a PhD candidate at the Department of Molecular and Cellular Sports Medicine, Institute of Cardiology and Sports Medicine, German Sport University Cologne, Germany, supervised by Prof. Wilhelm Bloch. I lead the LEAP trial on low energy availability in endurance masters athletes.
  • My research Citations510h-index8 addresses athlete health and performance across the lifespan through:
    • Athlete health and performance — energy availability and REDs
    • Ageing, sleep, and health behaviours
    • Exercise physiology and training monitoring
  • My work combines international cohort studies at the World Masters Athletics Championships, randomised crossover trials, and cell-culture experiments.
