Research Interests

  1. 01

    Athlete Health & Performance | Energy Availability & REDs

    Energy availability and Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (REDs), with a focus on masters, female, and endurance athletes — including the LEAP trial and the TaFMAC cohort.

  2. 02

    Ageing, Sleep & Health Behaviours

    How ageing, sleep, and health behaviours shape athlete health and performance — from post-COVID-19 condition and menopausal symptoms to athlete sleep screening.

  3. 03

    Exercise Physiology & Training Monitoring

    Time-efficient training such as exercise snacks, interventions to break up sedentary behaviour, fitness assessment, and molecular responses to exercise.

Research Projects

  • 2025 – present

    LEAP: Low Energy Availability in Performance Among Endurance Masters Athletes

    Project Lead · Funded by HIFF (University Internal Research Funding, German Sport University Cologne, ~€5,000) · DRKS00039418

    Registered randomised crossover trial on short-term low energy availability in endurance masters athletes aged 50–65, with metabolic, endocrine, performance, and psychological outcomes.

  • 2024 – present

    TaFMAC: Track and Field Masters Athletes Cohort Study

    Core Member; Lead Investigator of Sub-studies

    International cohort with on-site data collection at the 2024 World Masters Athletics Championships (Gothenburg); sub-studies on the IOC REDs CAT2 and women’s health in masters athletes.

  • 2025 – present

    MyoMaster: Myogenic Bioactivity of Masters Athletes’ Circulating Environment

    Lead Investigator · Sub-study of TaFMAC

    Does long-term exercise training shape a circulating environment that supports muscle regeneration? Using a human serum × C2C12 myoblast bioassay (Ki-67, Western blot, qPCR), we test whether serum from world-class masters athletes promotes myoblast proliferation and differentiation, and whether these effects differ by sex and sport discipline.

  • 2020 – 2025

    Mixed Exercise & Sedentary Behaviour Intervention

    Collaborator; Lead Investigator of Sub-study

    14-week rowing/cycling intervention in university employees; sub-study on sedentary-behaviour accumulation patterns.

  • 2022

    COVID@Uni: Health Behaviours During COVID-19 Campus Lockdown

    Project Lead

  • 2021 – 2022

    ASSQ-CN: Chinese Athlete Sleep Screening Questionnaire

    Project Lead

    Translation, cross-cultural adaptation, and accelerometer-based validation with the original developers (394 athletes).
